# Third-party notices

The repository structure is informed by the MIT-licensed
[`zig-utils/zig-starter`](https://github.com/zig-utils/zig-starter) project.
The npm native-binary distribution layout is informed by the MIT-licensed
[`ziex-dev/zigc`](https://github.com/ziex-dev/zigc) project.

APCA is intentionally not implemented. Its reference-code license limits
royalty-free use to web-accessibility contrast, requires commercial or
paywalled integrations to provide free audit access, requires ports to stay
synchronized with the current approved algorithm, and restricts use of the APCA
name. APCA also remains a candidate method for the unfinished WCAG 3 standard.
Brand Peel CLI uses WCAG 2 contrast ratios instead.
